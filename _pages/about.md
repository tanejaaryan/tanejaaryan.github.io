I am a Software Engineer at [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com) (Open Connect Foundations and Infrastructure) in Los Gatos, working on high-throughput serving systems. I completed my M.S. in Computer Science (Research track) at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in May 2026, advised by [Prof. Deepak Vasisht](https://deepakv.web.illinois.edu/). I also received the [J.N TATA Scholarship for Higher Education](https://jntataendowment.org), and in 2025 I was selected as one of 200 [Heidelberg Young Researchers](https://www.heidelberg-laureate-forum.org/).

Previously, I interned at Netflix (Open Connect Transport R&D), working on transport protocol performance. Thanks to my mentors [Renata Teixeira](https://www.linkedin.com/in/renata-teixeira-383979258), [Reese Enghardt](https://tenghardt.net/about/about/), and [Grenville Armitage](https://www.linkedin.com/in/grenville-armitage/)! Before that, I worked at [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-india/) for ~2 years with [Venkat Padmanabhan](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/padmanab/), [Debopam Bhattacherjee](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/debopamb/), and [Rohan Gandhi](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/rohangandhi/). I have also interned at [MathWorks](https://www.mathworks.com) in the Engineering Development Group.

My work spans distributed systems, networking, and systems for LLMs. More specifically, I have worked on transport and congestion control, efficient LLM serving (including energy-efficient inference), and edge computing for video streaming.

Before joining Microsoft, I graduated from IIIT-Delhi with a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering. I worked with [Prof. Arani Bhattacharya](https://faculty.iiitd.ac.in/~arani/), [Prof. Sanjit Kaul](https://sites.google.com/view/sanjitkkaul/), [Prof. Koteswar Rao](https://sites.google.com/site/koteswarraojerripothula/) and [Prof. Mukulika Maity](https://faculty.iiitd.ac.in/~mukulika/) for my undergraduate research & thesis.
